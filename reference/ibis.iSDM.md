# ibis.iSDM

Integrated framework of modelling the distribution of species and
ecosystems in a suitability framing. This package allows the estimation
of integrated species distribution models (iSDM) based on several
sources of evidence and provided presence-only and presence-absence
datasets. It makes heavy use of point-process models for estimating
habitat suitability and allows to include spatial latent effects and
priors in the estimation. To do so 'ibis.iSDM' supports a number of
engines for Bayesian and more non-parametric machine learning
estimation. Further, the 'ibis.iSDM' is specifically customized to
support spatial-temporal projections of habitat suitability into the
future.
